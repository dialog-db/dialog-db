## 2026-11-13, Diagnosing to Designing: what main writes, and an approach

**Asked.** The maintainer asked for the optimizations the research topic found plausible to be done soundly. For this one that meant first: can the firing for an untouched entity ever write something the narrower seed does not, or is it only work repeated?

**Learned.**

- It writes. A command is issued again and whatever acts on it runs again; a durable head under `last` writes its value over what was written since (claim 3).
- Main is not consistent with itself: a commit of two fields of an entity finds that entity alone, a commit of one finds every holder of the value, and the crate's own differential check of the two fails where a test reaches such a commit (claim 4).
- Binding the seed alone would lose a match main finds by accident: an attribute derived from itself never counted as changed derivedly, so a rule over it was seeded, and the value alone happened on the derived facts (claim 5). The same rule with a named subject misses them on main.

**Decided.** Not decided: it is a change in what commits write. The approach built binds the seed and counts a written attribute as derived when a deductive rule concludes it from something touched. It rests on an opinion (claim 10) and leaves one hypothesis open (claim 9).
