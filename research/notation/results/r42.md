## What it shows

Saying what a line is, a claim that is read when its concept is asked for and concluded when its concept is concluded, brings the note back to R39's level. Readers now walk the rule through the lines: "The rule concludes the `memo` lines for `mail:one`. One of those lines is `xyz.tonk.post/content of: mail:one is: "hello world"`". R41's "reads the claims of one attribute" let readers take a concept for a read-only view and match concepts by name.

The one miss kept the name: "the query asks for `note`… nothing stores or derives it".
