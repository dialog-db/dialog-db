- `?x` is a variable. `(task/status ?t is: :done)` is the fact (?t, task/status, :done).
- `(rule effects... (when premises...))`: whenever the premises match, the effects happen.
- Effect `(assert (x ...))` makes x hold in the same step. Effect `(assert! (x ...))` makes x hold from the next step on. Effect `(retract! (x ...))` removes x from the next step on.

(rule (assert! (task/status ?t is: :archived))
