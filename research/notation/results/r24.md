## Not in the rubric

**The clash with the submission mode.** No `query` reader called `(query …)` beside "submitted as a query" a problem; all five gave it as a reason to keep `query`, since the form and the mode share a name. All fifteen readers of the other words gave the opposite: `query` would collide with the submission mode. Readers who have the clash do not see it; readers who do not have it name it.

**`?` and variables.** No `mark` reader confused `(? …)` with a variable; one gave its matching the `?` of variables as a reason to keep it.

**What readers named as most confusing.** Thirteen of twenty: that the document never shows where `!` goes on a rule head, so task 15 was a guess; eleven of them proposed a word instead, `(next …)` or `(later …)`. Seven: that one shape, `(todo …)`, is a pattern in a question, a command's shape, and an `assert`'s target, told apart only by what wraps it; five proposed marking commands, `(do add …)` or `(issue add …)`.
