# R39: the framing, a one-field example and the `last` sentence are enough

2026-11-13 (UTC). 120 readers on the clean runner (`claude-haiku-5-5`), single turn. Scored by the rubric in [r39-plan](r39-plan.md); every answer is in [`r39.jsonl`](r39.jsonl). Question 8 on the `todo` document was checked by hand where the pattern match was unsure.

Against the bars, every cell works except `framing` on the to-do, which lands between them.

Predictions:

- **`expansion`, held.** It repeated R38.
- **`framing-example`, held.** It works as well as `expansion`.
- **`framing`, wrong on `note`.** I expected the framing alone to land between the bars on both documents. It did on the to-do, but on the note it scored 20 of 20.

## What it shows

**The election is settled by the `last` sentence.** In R38, 9 of 16 readers who saw the rename land said the to-do had two titles. Here every reader who saw the rename land said it shows "Fix login bug", for dialog's reason: "the asserted 'Fix login bug' is newer than the concluded value, so `last` picks it". Every reader then said a later issue change shows "Login fails".

**The framing alone works when the rule concludes a concept and another concept reads it.** On `note`, a rule concludes `memo` and `note` reads the attribute. 20 of 20 readers got that from "a concept is just a set of attributes".

**It does not reach a domain call in another rule's condition.** In the to-do, rule B reads `(xyz.todo ?t title: ?old)`, and half the readers still said B "needs a stored `xyz.todo` title". One gave the exact reason: "A concludes `todo`, not `xyz.todo`".

**The one-field example closes that.** It shows `(rule (titled ...) ...)` "is the same as" `(rule (xyz.acme.task ?t title: ?title) ...)`, and with it the to-do reached 18 of 20. The longer two-field expansion did no better (19).

One confound: the `last` sentence speaks of values "concluded by a rule" for an attribute, which may itself carry part of the model. Every variant has it, so the comparison between variants stands.

## What follows

The spec needs three things, in this order:

1. The framing: a concept is just a set of attributes that share an entity, a convenient way to express derivations over those attributes more concisely.
2. The one-field example, a concept rule and the domain rule it is the same as.
3. The election: `last` picks the value caused most recently, stored or concluded, and a concluded value is as recent as the newest fact it was concluded from.

That is short enough for the opening of the design, and it is what design.md should say.
