## Against the bars

- **Reads right:** both medians at least 13 of 15. Yes.
- **Clarity improves:** no. Neither median rating reached 4, and in both documents more than 2 of 5 readers still name `!`.
- **`assert` beats `short`:** no. Its median rating is the same, and one reader fewer names `!`, where the bar asked for two. By the plan, keep `todo!`.
- **The decisions read:** no `!` in a premise, 10 of 10; item 12, 10 of 10; item 11 fails in `short` at 2 of 5 and passes in `assert` at 4 of 5.

## Item 11 is a hole in the card

The four readers who missed it all gave one reason: `assert` is a modifier, the card says a modifier's facts are tagged so a concept query never sees them, so a `(todo …)` query cannot see what `(assert (todo …))` derives. That follows from the card as written. It says `assert`'s bug "holds in this tick" in one place and that modifier facts never reach a concept query in another, and never says which wins. Only the prelude, which neither document had, says `assert` is the modifier whose facts become the concept's. In `assert`, where every concept write is an `assert`, three of four readers resolved it the intended way.

## Not in the rubric

**What readers named as most confusing.** `!` still leads, 7 of 10, but no longer for the readings R17 named. Premises are gone from the complaints. What is left:

- A top-level `!` decides whether a run commits, while a head's `!` means "into the next tick". Readers see two jobs: "whether a `!` rule head is stored depends on the outer form, not on the rule".
- On a modifier `!` means the next tick only; on `assert!` it means stored.

Content identity is named first by 3 of 10 (1 of 10 in R17): an `assert` with no entity derives it from every field, so changing `state` makes another to-do, and nothing at the call site says so.

What readers proposed:

| proposal | readers |
|---|---|
| a word for the next tick, `(next (add …))` or `next:` | 7 |
| a word for storing or committing, `(store …)`, `(commit …)`, `!` for commit only | 5 |
| a word for an uncommitted run, `(preview …)` | 1 |
| name the identifying fields: `entity: (id list: … text: …)`, `(key list text)`, `id:` | 4 |
