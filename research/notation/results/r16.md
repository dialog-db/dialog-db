## Not in the rubric

**The notice's entity.** Task 7 sends a `notice` the premises give no entity for. Every reader of `card`, `tutorial` and `pitfalls` (15 of 15) left it out, as their `ensue!` example does. Four of five `spec` readers reused the to-do's entity, `(notice ?t todo: ?t …)`, and one derived one with `digest`; the spec's `ensue!` example passes a bound `?r`. Reusing `?t` puts the notice's facts on the to-do. The documents do not say what a rule head that leaves its entity out lands on.
