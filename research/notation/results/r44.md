## What it shows

**Readers read the composite without help.** Every reader read a query with no `at:` as covering every layer, with the pick electing across them, and a bound `at:` as that layer alone.

**Left to themselves, they retract what they see.** 18 of 20 `bare` readers said retracting "Call mom tonight" removes it: "the retract removes the session claim with that exact title". The 2 who did not reasoned that a retraction defaults like an assert. 6 of 20 then wrote the same unscoped retraction to remove the title.

**One sentence fixes it.** "A write with `at:` left out goes to the primary, an assert and a retraction alike" took question 3 to 20 of 20: "Primary has no 'Call mom tonight' claim, so the retraction removes nothing". Every reader then wrote `(retract … at: :session)`.

**The image-editor line adds nothing measurable**, at the ceiling. Readers echoed it ("erases the session claim"), so it does no harm.
